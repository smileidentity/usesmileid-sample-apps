No account, credentials or special access are needed. Every screen is reachable on a fresh install.

To see the token session states, open the scan sheet from the products screen and choose "Simulate a successful scan": it mints a fixture token, links a session, and shows the countdown and the flow handoff. No real token or partner exists behind it.

Verification runs go to the Smile ID sandbox. Starting a product opens the SDK's camera flow, which is where the camera prompt appears.

Location and photo-library usage strings are declared because the SDK references those APIs; this app never requests either permission, and no location or photo prompt appears in normal use.

On devices that support face tracking, the selfie screen runs an ARKit face-tracking session and reads two blend shape coefficients, mouthSmileLeft and mouthSmileRight, averaged into one smile probability between 0.0 and 1.0. That number times the shutter, so a live person has to smile rather than hold up a photograph. It is held in memory for the lifetime of the screen and cleared when the screen closes. No face mesh, depth map or blend shape data is stored or transmitted, and the ARKit face mesh is created invisible purely to keep tracking alive. What the app uploads is the same on every device, with or without a TrueDepth camera: JPEG photographs of the selfie and a few liveness frames, sent to the Smile ID verification service. Face data handling is described in the privacy policy at https://smile.id/privacy-policy, sections 2, 3.4, 5 and 9.

The TrueDepth camera is used by that selfie step only. Five of the six products include it (SmartSelfie Enrollment, SmartSelfie Authentication, Biometric KYC, Document Verification, Enhanced Document Verification); Enhanced KYC has no camera step. ARKit data is not used to authenticate anyone: SmartSelfie Authentication compares the uploaded JPEG selfie with a previously enrolled one on Smile ID's servers.

Audience: Smile ID is a business-to-business identity verification provider and this is the reference app for its mobile SDKs. It is for developers and decision makers at Smile ID's enterprise customers evaluating the SDK before integrating it, and for Smile ID's own sales and solutions teams demonstrating it. It is not aimed at consumers or children. Linking the app to a customer's own Smile ID account by scanning a session code from the Smile ID Portal is optional; without one, runs go to the sandbox and no account is needed.
